# FAIR-style methodology notes

FAIR-style analysis models loss-event frequency and loss magnitude for a defined scenario. Inputs need provenance, units, period, scope, uncertainty and owner. Separate measured values from expert estimates and derived values.

Simulation is conditional on its inputs and model; it does not independently validate them. Document distribution choices, dependencies, seed, iterations, convergence diagnostics, sensitivity and exclusions. Avoid double counting secondary loss, reputation, churn, legal costs and regulatory outcomes.

The FAIR Institute analyst guidance referenced by the supplied package was marked as a draft for comment; treat it as orientation, not a normative standard. Verify current primary references before formal use.
