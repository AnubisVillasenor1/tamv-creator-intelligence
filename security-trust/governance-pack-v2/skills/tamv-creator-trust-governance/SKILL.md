# Creator trust governance
Assess purpose, data provenance, affected groups, access, inference, recommendation effects, monetization, abuse, appeal and remedy. Separate aggregate metrics from identifiable records. Do not infer consent from account activity or a hash. A parity metric is not a universal fairness verdict.

For each proposal record affected parties, purpose, data fields, source/rights, retention, model/provider, plausible harms, observability, contestability, remedy, owner and review date.

Escalate profiling, minors, sensitive inferences, financial impacts and cross-border flows to privacy, legal and human review. This skill drafts assessments; it does not approve deployment or certify fairness.
